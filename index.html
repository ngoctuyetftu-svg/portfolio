About

I like turning many moving parts into one finished result.

My first real project started with an idea. As a Workplace Services intern at Intel Products Vietnam, I proposed and led an onboarding video for Corporate Services, with zero budget, writing, filming, acting in and editing it myself. It replaced a 60-minute briefing with a 5-minute video and Q&A, and the team still uses it today. That was when I found out how much I enjoy pulling ideas, people and details together into something finished.

At VNG, I joined the Employer Branding team and ran programs for students and universities, plus internal events at a company of about 4,000 people. Each one asked for something different: planning, budgeting, communication, logistics, online and offline. That variety pulled me toward program and project management, and toward people experience work.

Six months in, I was asked to lead University Week 2026, one of the team's key annual programs. Leading it taught me what a project lead needs to see: the whole picture, who needs to be involved, and which touchpoints keep everyone moving toward the goal. The program beat its KPIs and was recognized by my manager at the mid-year review.

Every piece of work counts, if not for the result, then for the lesson.
