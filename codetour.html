2026 · Finalist care

Getting 24 finalists to Ho Chi Minh City

51 finalists were selected for the offline final, and 24 of them travelled from outside HCMC.

  • Hosted the finalist dinner the night before the final.
  • Kept finalists updated through a Zalo group and automated emails built with Power Automate.
