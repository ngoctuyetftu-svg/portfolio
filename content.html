Part 02 · My own project · 2024

An English-learning TikTok channel

@meowlearnsenglish · Creator

  • Goal: get people excited about English and keep them interested for the long run.
  • Approach: a “studying without knowing” content strategy, with English lessons hidden inside funny, relatable posts about everyday life and trending news.
  • Result: 5,000+ followers in the first 3 months and 100,000+ likes. The most viral post passed 500,000 interactions (views, likes, comments and shares).
